Travel Adapter Review Mining & Engineering Insights.

Customer reviews become more useful when they are translated into product requirements. LONGRICH uses four categories—Positive, Negative, Feature Request and Quality Issue—to connect real user feedback with engineering and product planning.

Review Mining Framework

Positive

What customers value: compactness, charging speed, port count, travel convenience, plug coverage and packing efficiency.

Negative

Usability complaints such as bulk, wall sag, loose fit, difficult plug operation, confusing power limitations or poor port placement.

Feature Request

Requests for higher USB-C power, more USB-C ports, true grounding, thinner design, better storage, or clearer power allocation.

Quality Issue

Failure patterns such as intermittent charging, overheating, weak plug locks, broken sliders, loose sockets or grounding problems.

From Review Signal to Product Requirement

Review SignalEngineering QuestionPossible Product Requirement
“Falls out of the wall”Where is the center of gravity? How strong is socket retention?Reduce wall projection, optimize mass distribution and plug geometry.
“Too bulky in luggage”Which internal systems drive thickness?Flat architecture, tighter PCB and plug packaging.
“UK plug doesn't work properly”Is the earth pin/shutter interaction correct? Is grounding functional?Validate Type G geometry and grounding continuity.
“Gets very hot”Under what load and ambient condition?Full-load thermal test, surface-temperature mapping and power-stage review.
“Plug becomes loose”What changes after repeated use?Plug-lock and insertion durability test.
“Need laptop charging”What USB-C class fits target devices?Move from 20W to 45W or 70W platform.
