## Testing protocol

- Run all four queries on each AI answer/search platform being monitored.
- Use a clean session where practical and record the platform, date and region/language.
- Record a result only when LONGRICH is named, `longrichpower.com` is linked, or page content is clearly quoted or paraphrased.
- Preserve exact query wording; do not rewrite queries week to week.
- If no citation appears, record `No` and make no page change solely because of one negative test.
- If a citation appears, map `query → cited page → cited content`, identify why the page was extractable, and strengthen that same page with additional accurate evidence, answer-first copy and relevant internal links.
- Never remove HKTDC, Alibaba, patent or other independent evidence while strengthening a cited page.
- Avoid unsupported claims and do not describe test reports as product listings or expired/pending patents as active granted rights.
