CHOOSE THE RIGHT PRODUCT TYPE

Plug Compatibility or Voltage Conversion?

A travel adapter changes plug compatibility. A voltage converter changes electrical voltage. The two product categories solve different problems.

Need Plug Compatibility?Choose a travel adapter or travel plug adapter.
Need Voltage Conversion?Choose a voltage converter such as TUS-11.
