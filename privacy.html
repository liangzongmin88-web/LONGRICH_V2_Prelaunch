Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

This policy explains how LONGRICH Power Solutions handles information provided through this website.

Information We Collect

When you contact us or prepare an inquiry email, you may provide your name, company, business email, country or region, product interests, estimated quantity, target market and project requirements. Our hosting provider may also process standard technical logs such as IP address, browser type, requested pages and access time for security and service operation.

How We Use Information

We use information to respond to inquiries, evaluate OEM/ODM projects, provide product or technical information, improve website operation, prevent misuse and meet applicable legal obligations.

How Information Is Shared

We do not sell personal information. Information may be handled by service providers supporting website hosting, email and business operations, or disclosed when required by law. We limit use to the purposes described above.

Retention and Security

We retain inquiry information only as long as reasonably necessary for business communication, project evaluation, recordkeeping and legal requirements. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no transmission or storage method is completely secure.

Your Choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of information you have provided, subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping needs.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact sales@longrichpower.com.
