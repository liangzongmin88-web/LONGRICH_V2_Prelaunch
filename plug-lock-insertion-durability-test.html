LONGRICH RELIABILITY ENGINEERING

Plug Lock & Insertion Durability Test.

Travel adapter plug mechanisms must deploy, lock and retract repeatedly without excessive looseness, sticking or loss of electrical and mechanical function. This page explains how LONGRICH structures that reliability evaluation.

What the Test Evaluates

Locking Reliability

Check whether the deployed plug stays securely in its intended position during normal use.

Insertion Durability

Repeat insertion and removal to identify wear, loosening, deformation or abnormal friction.

Retention Consistency

Compare plug feel before and after repeated cycles and verify that the mechanism remains usable.

Post-Cycle Function

Recheck AC continuity, grounding where applicable, shutter behavior and physical condition after cycling.

Typical Test Sequence

01

Baseline

Record initial plug movement, locking feel and visible condition.

02

Cycle

Operate the plug mechanism through the defined insertion/retraction sequence.

03

Inspect

Check pins, sliders, springs, stops, guides and housing for wear.

04

Retest

Recheck lock engagement, smooth travel and plug position.

05

Electrical Check

Confirm electrical and grounding continuity where relevant.

What Should Be Recorded

  • Cycle count
  • Plug type under test
  • Insertion and retraction method
  • Initial and final locking condition
  • Visible wear or deformation
  • Abnormal looseness, sticking or noise
  • Post-cycle electrical continuity
  • Post-cycle grounding continuity where applicable
Current public data status: LONGRICH has not published a confirmed cycle-count result for this platform yet. This page describes the engineering method and reporting framework only.

Why This Matters for B2B Buyers

A plug mechanism that feels acceptable on a new sample can degrade after repeated use. Buyers should ask suppliers to show both initial function and post-cycle condition rather than relying on a one-time demonstration.
