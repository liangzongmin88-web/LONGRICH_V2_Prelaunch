# Weekly Off-Page Exposure Playbook

Target: 5–10 qualified, relevant exposure actions per week. Quality and buyer relevance outrank raw link count. Never buy bulk links, automate forum spam, or claim certifications/patents that cannot be verified.

## Weekly mix

| Channel | Weekly target | Acceptable action |
|---|---:|---|
| Supplier/manufacturer directories | 1–2 | Complete or improve a real company/product profile with consistent NAP and a relevant landing-page link |
| Industry associations and trade media | 1 | Pitch a technical contribution, supplier profile, test-method note or interview |
| Distributor/importer partner pages | 1–2 | Request a factual manufacturer/brand listing or co-authored product guide |
| Engineering/resource citations | 1–2 | Offer a useful diagram, checklist, test explanation or data-backed expert quote |
| Relevant Q&A/community participation | 1–2 | Answer the question fully; link only when the page adds material detail and community rules allow it |
| Trade-show/exhibitor/vendor pages | 0–2 | Claim accurate exhibitor or supplier listings tied to real participation |

## Qualification gate

Record domain, page/topic relevance, estimated audience, editorial standard, link type, destination URL, contact/status and result. Reject link farms, unrelated guest-post networks, sitewide footer links, spun content, fake review sites and any placement requiring misleading identity or claims.

## KPI

Track qualified placements, referral sessions, engaged sessions, RFQ assists, new referring domains and branded/non-branded impressions. Do not report outreach attempts as earned links.
