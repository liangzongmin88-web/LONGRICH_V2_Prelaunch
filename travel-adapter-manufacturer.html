OEM and ODM Capability

OEM projects can start from existing LONGRICH platforms for branding, color, packaging and confirmed configuration changes. ODM projects can move deeper into industrial design, plug layout, grounding, compact architecture, USB-C power, tooling, testing and pilot production.

Engineering Areas That Matter

Plug Architecture

Pin geometry, locking, tolerances, multi-country plug layout and wall-socket stability.

Grounding & Safety

Protective-earth continuity, safety shutters, flame-retardant materials and product claims.

Power & Thermal

USB-C power delivery, PCB layout, thermal management and 20W/45W/70W product positioning.

Why Product Architecture Matters

Travel adapters combine plug mechanisms, AC power paths, USB charging electronics and safety features inside a compact enclosure. LONGRICH’s product direction emphasizes flatter proportions, efficient plug layouts, easier packing and better weight distribution rather than simply adding more ports.
