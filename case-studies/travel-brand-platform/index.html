ANONYMOUS CASE · TRAVEL BRAND · MULTI-MARKET

One product ladder instead of disconnected adapter ideas

A travel-focused brand was comparing multiple concepts without clear roles for each SKU. The project reorganized the range around compact, grounded and premium GaN use cases.

PlatformsNT009 / NT010-G / NT011
MarketsMulti-market
ScopePortfolio architecture
StagePlatform shortlist

The brief

The buyer wanted a coherent travel power family that could address entry-level charging, grounded applications and laptop-class USB-C use without creating overlapping offers.

What was aligned

  • 20W compact role for the entry tier
  • Grounded platform for continuity-led use cases
  • 70W GaN role for premium charging
  • Shared brand, color and packaging principles

Project checkpoint

The output was a three-tier shortlist with separate market and validation questions for each platform, ready for sample planning.

Why it mattered

Defining a role for every platform gave the brand a clearer buying story and prevented feature duplication across the proposed range.

Customer name, commercial terms and program dates are withheld. The case describes project decisions, not market-performance results.
