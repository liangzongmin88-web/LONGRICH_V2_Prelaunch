ANONYMOUS CASE · E-COMMERCE · UNITED STATES

A sample-ready brief for a 70W GaN travel adapter

A US-focused online seller needed to turn a broad “premium universal charger” concept into a sourcing brief that engineering, packaging and compliance teams could evaluate against the NT011-US platform.

PlatformNT011-US
MarketUnited States
ScopePrivate label
StageSample validation

The brief

The initial request prioritized 70W USB-C charging, a compact global plug system and a retail-ready private-label presentation. The buyer also needed a clear list of files and decisions required before ordering samples.

What was aligned

  • NT011-US as the evaluation platform
  • Port mix, destination market and target use
  • Logo position, color direction and packaging inputs
  • Documentation review tied to the final configuration

Project checkpoint

The output was a sample-ready specification and artwork checklist, separating product validation from later commercial confirmation of MOQ, pricing and production timing.

Why it mattered

Locking the market and configuration first reduced ambiguity in the sample request and gave both teams one reference for product, packaging and documentation review.

Customer name, order quantities, pricing and dates are withheld for confidentiality. No sales-performance claim is made.
