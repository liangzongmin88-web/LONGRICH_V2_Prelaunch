ANONYMOUS CASE · DISTRIBUTOR · EUROPEAN UNION

A focused EU assortment built around market and document fit

An importer began with a broad list across travel adapters, power strips and tower formats. The evaluation created a smaller sample shortlist by making market ratings and listed documentation explicit.

CategoriesThree
MarketEuropean Union
ScopeRange + document matrix
StageSample shortlist

The brief

The buyer needed complementary products for one regional channel, with fewer duplicate functions and a clearer view of the documentation listed for each model.

What was aligned

  • EU plug, socket and rated-power fit
  • Category role and overlap across SKUs
  • Listed CE / RoHS documentation by model
  • MOQ, packaging and sample questions

Project checkpoint

The output was a three-category shortlist and a model-level question set for documentation review before commercial quotation.

Why it mattered

Separating listed documents from final applicability helped keep the buying discussion precise and prevented assumptions based only on category-level claims.

Customer name, pricing, volumes and dates are withheld. Certification applicability remains subject to final product configuration and destination requirements.
