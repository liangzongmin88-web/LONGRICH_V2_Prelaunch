## Evidence governance

1. Assign an internal owner for factory claims, certification records, technical tests, and case-study approval.
2. Store the source document, applicable SKU/model, issuing body, validity date, and public-use approval for each certification asset.
3. Add a revision date and genuine team reviewer to technical resources only after review actually occurs.
4. Never publish customer names, production volumes, ratings, certifications, or personnel credentials without verifiable authorization.
5. Re-audit trust evidence quarterly and whenever a product platform or certificate changes.
