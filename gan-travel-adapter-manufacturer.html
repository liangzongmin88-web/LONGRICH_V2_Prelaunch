FAQ

GaN travel adapter sourcing questions.

What GaN travel adapter power levels does LONGRICH offer?

Current platform directions include 45W and 70W USB-C GaN travel adapters. Final output and power allocation depend on the selected model.

Can LONGRICH private-label a GaN travel adapter?

Existing platforms can be evaluated for logo, color, finish, packaging and supported configuration changes. Differentiated architecture can use an ODM path.

Which certifications apply?

Requirements depend on destination market and exact build. FCC, CE and RoHS requirements are reviewed project by project; a platform reference is not universal coverage.

Can a 70W platform charge laptops?

Yes, for compatible USB-C PD laptops when the laptop input, cable, per-port profile and multi-port power allocation match.

Does GaN automatically make the adapter cooler?

No. Enclosure size, PCB layout, component placement, load distribution and thermal paths still determine real product temperature.

What belongs in the RFQ?

Include market, power and ports, plug system, target devices, grounding, branding and packaging, quantity, compliance scope and launch date.
