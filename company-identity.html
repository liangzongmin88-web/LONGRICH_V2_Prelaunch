LONGRICH Power Solutions

LONGRICH Power Solutions on longrichpower.com is the B2B power-product manufacturing identity of Dongguan LongRich Electronic Co., Ltd. The company develops and manufactures travel adapters, voltage converters, power strips and related power products for OEM, ODM, private-label and distribution programs.
