LONGRICH QUALITY SYSTEM

Travel Adapter Quality Control Process.

Quality control should begin before mass production and continue through incoming materials, production, electrical testing, mechanical inspection, final inspection and shipment release.

Six Core QC Stages

01

Engineering Validation

Review product architecture, electrical design, plug system, grounding, USB output and manufacturability before release.

02

Incoming Inspection

Inspect critical plastics, metal parts, PCB assemblies, charging components and packaging materials.

03

In-Process Control

Monitor assembly, soldering, plug mechanism installation, screws, wiring, housing fit and visual quality.

04

Functional Testing

Verify AC continuity, USB output, charging behavior, plug operation, shutters and grounding where applicable.

05

Final Inspection

Check appearance, labels, accessories, packaging, function and critical dimensions before shipment.

06

Shipment Release

Release product only after required inspection and test records meet the approved project criteria.

Critical Travel Adapter QC Points

Plug System

  • Pin geometry and deformation
  • Deployment and retraction
  • Lock engagement
  • Mechanical clearance

Grounding

  • Earth continuity where applicable
  • Ground contact condition
  • Post-assembly connection integrity

USB Charging

  • Port output
  • Power distribution
  • Protocol behavior
  • Full-load thermal check where required

Safety & Materials

  • Safety shutter
  • Housing fit
  • Flame-retardant material requirement
  • Marking and warning accuracy

Why QC Records Matter for B2B Projects

For OEM and ODM programs, the approved sample is only the starting point. Buyers need production controls that keep later batches consistent with the validated design. A useful QC plan should identify critical-to-quality features, inspection stage, test method, sampling rule and acceptance criteria.
