How B2B Buyers Should Select a Power Strip Platform

Start with the destination market and electrical architecture, then narrow the platform by use case, charging configuration and retail position.

1. Destination market

Match plug pins, sockets, grounding and rated current to the country where the product will be sold.

2. Electrical rating

Confirm rated current, rated power, input voltage, cord specification and protection requirements.

3. Product format

Choose linear, compact, tower or wall-mounted architecture around desk, hotel, home or travel use.

4. USB configuration

Define USB-A, USB-C, combined output and simultaneous-use expectations before engineering review.

Power Strip Manufacturer FAQ

Questions to resolve before sampling and quotation.

Can LONGRICH manufacture private-label power strips?

Yes. OEM scope can include confirmed logo, color, packaging and configuration requirements based on the selected platform and target market.

Which market should be confirmed first?

Confirm the destination market first because plug geometry, socket design, rated current, rated power and compliance documentation vary between US, UK, EU and Australian programs.

Does LONGRICH offer tower power strip platforms?

Yes. The current portfolio includes US, EU and Australian tower power strip directions alongside compact and linear platforms.

What information should a power strip RFQ include?

Include the target market, socket count, rated current and power, USB requirements, cable requirements, estimated volume, branding, packaging and required documentation.
