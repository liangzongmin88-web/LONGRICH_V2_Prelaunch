ENGINEERING

Travel Adapter Design Is More Than Adding USB Ports

Travel adapters combine mechanical plug systems, AC power, charging electronics and safety structures inside a compact enclosure. Product performance depends on how these systems work together.

  • Plug geometry and tolerances
  • Center of gravity
  • Grounding path
  • Safety shutter
  • Plug locking mechanism
  • Flame-retardant materials
  • PCB layout
  • Thermal management

COMPACT ARCHITECTURE

Compact and Flat Travel Adapter Development

A travel adapter that projects farther from the wall creates more leverage on the plug connection. Product thickness, weight, plug geometry, socket retention and internal weight distribution can all affect stability.

LONGRICH develops compact product architectures with efficient internal layouts and plug arrangements to reduce unnecessary thickness, improve portability and support better weight distribution.

Engineering Factors

  • Product weight and thickness
  • Plug length and geometry
  • Socket retention force
  • Internal component placement
  • Center of gravity
  • Travel and luggage portability

Actual wall-socket stability depends on the complete product design and the condition of the destination socket.

Grounded Travel Adapter Development

A grounded travel adapter requires a properly designed grounding path through the product rather than simply adding an external third pin.

  • Input ground pin
  • Internal ground connection
  • Output ground contact
  • Electrical continuity
  • Mechanical reliability
  • Plug-system compatibility

FAQ

OEM & ODM Travel Adapter Questions

Direct answers to common questions from brand owners and sourcing teams.

What is the MOQ for OEM travel adapters?

LONGRICH's typical MOQ starts from 1,000 pieces. Exact MOQ depends on the model, packaging and customization requirements.

Can LONGRICH develop a completely new travel adapter?

Yes. ODM development can cover product architecture, industrial design, mechanical engineering, electrical development, prototyping, tooling, testing and mass production.

Can USB-C charging power be customized?

Different product platforms can support different charging architectures, including approximately 20W, 45W and 70W classes depending on the final product design and thermal requirements.

Can you develop a grounded travel adapter?

Grounded configurations can be evaluated according to the plug system, product structure, intended equipment and destination market.

How long does an ODM project take?

Development time depends on product complexity, tooling, validation, certification and final requirements. A project schedule is defined after the product specification is confirmed.

Can you manufacture from our existing design?

Yes. Customers can provide samples, drawings or technical requirements for engineering and manufacturability evaluation.

Do travel adapters convert voltage?

Normally, no. A travel adapter changes plug compatibility but does not automatically convert voltage. The connected device must support the destination voltage unless a separate voltage-conversion function is included.
