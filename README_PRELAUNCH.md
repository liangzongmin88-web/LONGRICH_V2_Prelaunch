## Before public launch
1. Replace preview-only RFQ fields with a real form backend or CRM.
2. Verify the WhatsApp QR code and add a direct mobile WhatsApp link if desired.
3. Review Privacy Policy and Terms of Use with your legal/compliance team.
4. Verify every published product specification against the latest approved product data.
5. Confirm product images are the intended production versions.
6. Set the custom domain `longrichpower.com`.
7. Enable HTTPS.
8. Submit `/sitemap.xml` to Google Search Console.
