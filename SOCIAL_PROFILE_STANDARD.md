## Evidence preservation rules

- Keep the HKTDC sourcing profile and fair-catalogue references.
- Keep the Alibaba supplier presence as historical third-party evidence; do not copy unverified revenue or market-share figures.
- Keep public patent references with publication number and status. Do not describe expired or pending rights as active granted patents.
- Preserve independent evidence separately from first-party factory claims.
- Do not replace these evidence references when shortening a social bio; move them to the platform's About, Details or Featured section instead.
