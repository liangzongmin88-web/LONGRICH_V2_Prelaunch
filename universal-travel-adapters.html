IMPORTANT PRODUCT DISTINCTION

A Travel Adapter Does Not Convert Voltage.

Universal travel adapters solve plug compatibility and USB charging requirements. They do not change mains voltage.

If the connected appliance does not support the destination voltage, choose a true voltage converter instead.
