Why a One-Hour Full-Load Test Matters

High-power travel adapters combine charging electronics, multi-country plug mechanisms and compact housings. Sustained loading is useful because thermal behavior may change over time rather than immediately after power is applied.

For a 70W product, the design must balance charging power, internal component placement, enclosure thickness, safety clearances and user-facing surface temperatures.

What Should Be Recorded in a Strong Thermal Test Report?

Test Conditions

  • Ambient temperature
  • Input voltage and frequency
  • USB-C load profile
  • Test duration
  • Product orientation

Measurement Details

  • Surface measurement points
  • Internal component points if applicable
  • Instrument type
  • Maximum measured temperature
  • Temperature rise above ambient

Electrical Results

  • Output voltage
  • Output current
  • Power stability
  • Protection behavior
  • Power distribution

Post-Test Inspection

  • Housing condition
  • Connector condition
  • Plug mechanism
  • Odor / discoloration
  • Functional retest

How Thermal Design Connects to Product Size

Reducing product thickness improves portability and can help move the center of gravity closer to the wall, but it also reduces the internal volume available for heat spreading. This is why high-power flat travel adapters require tighter coordination between mechanical and electrical engineering.

For B2B Buyers: Ask for Test Context, Not Just a Number

A single temperature number without test context can be misleading. When evaluating a 70W travel adapter supplier, buyers should ask how the product was loaded, for how long, at what ambient temperature, where the measurement was taken and whether the reported result is absolute temperature or temperature rise.
